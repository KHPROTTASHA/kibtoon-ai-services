Operational Intelligence

Questions we hear most.

Common tactical questions about our deployment protocols and field standards.

Do you integrate with our existing project management software?

Yes. Error.404 integrates with the scheduling and cost platforms most crews already run, so field data flows in without a rip-and-replace.

How does the AI handle weather and supply-chain disruptions?

The scheduling model re-sequences automatically around delays and flags the downstream trades affected, so your team isn't finding out on-site.

Does the safety monitoring replace our site supervisors?

No — it augments them. Cameras and sensors catch what's easy to miss across a large site; your supervisors still make the calls.

What happens if a project needs to pause mid-build?

Engagements are structured in clean phases with no lock-in, so monitoring and scheduling can scale down and resume without losing site history.

What's the typical timeline from site scan to breaking ground?

Most sites move from diagnostic scan to a live blueprint within two to four weeks, depending on site complexity and permitting.
